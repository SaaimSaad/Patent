A granted patent for a **Cropped Delta Wing** configuration developed for **VTOL
(Vertical Take-Off and Landing) drone systems**. The design targets aerodynamic
efficiency, structural optimization, and improved stability for fixed-wing VTOL
unmanned aerial vehicles.

This repository holds the patent certificate, the supporting research and design
report, and the source for the project's web page.

---

## Overview

Fixed-wing VTOL platforms must reconcile two conflicting flight regimes: efficient
cruise in fixed-wing mode and controllable, stable behaviour through the hover and
transition phases. The cropped delta wing geometry documented here is aimed at that
trade-off — preserving the lift-to-drag benefits of a delta planform while improving
transition stability and structural efficiency for UAV-scale airframes.
